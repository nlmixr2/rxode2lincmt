# Package index

## Linear compartment solutions

Analytic one, two and three compartment solutions and their building
blocks.

- [`linCmtModelDouble()`](https://nlmixr2.github.io/rxode2lincmt/reference/linCmtModelDouble.md)
  : Advance a linear compartment model by one step
- [`linCmtMicros()`](https://nlmixr2.github.io/rxode2lincmt/reference/linCmtMicros.md)
  : Micro-constants of a linear compartment model
- [`solComp2()`](https://nlmixr2.github.io/rxode2lincmt/reference/solComp2.md)
  : Eigenvalues and coefficients of the two compartment model
- [`solComp3()`](https://nlmixr2.github.io/rxode2lincmt/reference/solComp3.md)
  : Eigenvalues and coefficients of the three compartment model
